Target screen

Every screened Iowa county scored on the four things that decide whether a 640-acre footprint is buildable and gettable: landform, assemblage difficulty, ordinance status, and depth of the large-holding pool. Owners per section is 640 divided by the county median farm size — roughly how many signatures a full section needs. Counties where a live site sits are marked.

What these columns mean
Score0-100. Landform 30, assemblage 28, ordinance 27, pool depth 15. Higher is better.
Owners / sec640 ÷ the county's median farm size — how many signatures a full section needs. Lower is better. Under 3 is easy; over 6 is painful.
Farms ≥500How many farms in the county are 500 acres or larger. This is the size of the pool you are fishing in.
≥1,000Farms of 1,000 acres or more — the ones where a single owner could cover the whole footprint.
Median farmThe middle farm size in that county. Bigger median means fewer owners to deal with.
LandformIowa's terrain regions. Northwest Iowa Plains and Iowan Surface are the best; Southern Iowa Drift Plain is cut by streams and the worst for a slab.
OrdinanceWhether the county has closed itself to data centres. Closed and Drafting mean do not spend money there yet.
PositionWhether a live site is already on record in that county.

Owners per section is an optimistic floor — the census counts operations, and large operators farm rented ground across several owners. Calibrate against a real parcel dissolve before trusting it.

Packages

A package is a route to 500+ contiguous buildable acres. Three kinds: combine what is already on record, target ground that fits the screen but has not been found yet, or take a site somebody else already assembled. Acreage and owner counts are computed live from the pipeline and the county screen.

Gross and net acreage on combine packages is the sum of their component sites. Owners is the component count, or for target packages the county's owners-per-section figure rounded up. A package that cannot form one contiguous footprint says so — those are county positions, not campuses.

The one field that cannot be downloaded

No published list exists of which Iowa counties have countywide zoning. Roughly half do. What is documented: Iowa County is unzoned outside two-mile city rings and the Amana district, Washington County rescinded its ordinance in 2013, Chickasaw is reported unzoned, and the Lohrville listing records no zoning in Calhoun. Everything else is a phone call to the zoning administrator — county sites follow the pattern <county>county.iowa.gov. Each answer collected drops into counties.json and re-scores the screen.
