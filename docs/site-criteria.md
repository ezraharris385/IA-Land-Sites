# Site Criteria — Screen Before You Dial

The point of this screen is to make outreach efficient: every owner we call
should control land that already works on paper. Adjust thresholds as the
project definition firms up, but don't skip the screen.

## Hard requirements (kill criteria)

- **Transmission proximity.** Within ~1–2 miles of a 161 kV or 345 kV line,
  or within ~3 miles of an existing substation with expansion room. An energy
  campus without a realistic interconnection path is a farm.
- **Contiguity potential.** Target 300–800+ contiguous acres, either one
  ownership or an assemblage of 2–6 owners. More than ~8 owners in an
  assemblage and the holdout math gets ugly.
- **Buildable ground.** Outside the 100-year floodplain for the core campus
  footprint; slopes generally under 5%; no known karst/sinkhole areas.
- **Zoning path.** County zoning that is ag/industrial with a plausible path
  to a conditional use permit or rezone. Check whether the county has a
  utility-scale energy ordinance already (a fight someone else already had is
  a gift).
- **Road access.** Hard-surface county or state road frontage, or a short,
  feasible access build.

## Strong preferences (scoring criteria)

- **Lower-CSR2 ground.** Iowa prices farmland off CSR2. Parcels with CSR2 in
  the 50s–60s are meaningfully cheaper per acre than 85+ ground, and owners
  of lighter ground are more receptive to alternatives to row crops. Sort the
  parcel universe by CSR2 ascending — that's the motivated-seller list hiding
  in plain sight.
- **Absentee or aging ownership.** Out-of-state tax mailing addresses,
  estates, trusts, and owners 70+ with no obvious successor are the highest
  probability conversations.
- **Existing disturbance.** Former industrial sites, closed ethanol plants,
  gravel pits, rail-served ground, decommissioned generation sites — anything
  where "energy campus" is a smaller conceptual leap for neighbors and the
  board of supervisors.
- **Water and fiber.** Rural water district capacity or groundwater
  availability; long-haul fiber routes nearby (often follows rail and
  interstates).
- **Friendly county posture.** Counties with an economic development office
  actively recruiting projects, existing wind/solar/BESS installations, or a
  certified industrial site program.

## What to pre-build before outreach

For each candidate cluster, a one-page internal sheet:
parcel map with owners color-coded, acreage, CSR2, assessed value, distance
to transmission, floodplain overlay, and the assemblage sequence (which owner
is the anchor, who's optional, who's the risk).
