# Iowa Data Sources — Building the Parcel Universe for Free

## Parcel ownership & assessment

- **Beacon (Schneider Geospatial)** — beacon.schneidercorp.com — hosts
  parcel GIS + assessor data for a large share of Iowa's 99 counties.
  Owner name, tax mailing address, acreage, assessed value, often CSR2.
- **Vanguard Appraisals portals** — the other common county assessor
  platform (camavision-style sites); check each target county's assessor
  page for its portal link.
- **Iowa county GIS open-data portals** — many counties publish downloadable
  parcel shapefiles; the state's geospatial clearinghouse
  (geodata.iowa.gov) aggregates statewide layers.
- **County plat books** — Farm & Home Publishers county plat books; the
  paper artifact matters for windshield tours and coffee-shop conversations.

## Soil / land quality

- **CSR2 by parcel** — via assessor portals or ISU's Iowa Soil Properties
  and Interpretations Database (ISPAID); AcreValue also surfaces CSR2 and
  estimated values per parcel (free tier is enough for screening).
- **Web Soil Survey (USDA NRCS)** — soils, slope, drainage class.

## Transmission, energy, infrastructure

- **HIFLD / EIA energy mapping layers** — transmission lines (voltage
  classes), substations, and power plants; EIA's U.S. Energy Atlas has
  downloadable GIS layers.
- **MISO interconnection queue** — public queue reports show active/withdrawn
  projects by county — both a competition check and a lead source (withdrawn
  projects = landowners who already signed options once).
- **Utility service-territory maps** — Iowa Utilities Commission publishes
  electric service territory maps (MidAmerican, Alliant/IPL, RECs, munis).
- **FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer** — floodplain screen.
- **Fiber/rail** — long-haul fiber generally follows interstates and rail;
  rail layers via Iowa DOT GIS.

## Life-event and motivation signals

- **Iowa Courts Online (iowacourts.state.ia.us)** — probate case search by
  county; estates holding farmland.
- **County recorder** — recorded wind/solar easements and memoranda of
  option (search grantee names of known developers); expired instruments =
  pre-educated landowners.
- **County treasurer** — delinquent tax lists and annual June tax sale lists.
- **Auction results** — Sullivan Auctioneers, Steffes Group, and local
  auction house sites publish results; flag NO-SALE farmland auctions in
  target counties for immediate follow-up.
- **USDA FSA/CRP data** — county-level CRP enrollment and expiration data
  from USDA; expiring contracts force owner decisions.

## Market pricing references (for the uniform pricing framework)

- **ISU Extension Farmland Value Survey** — annual county-level $/acre.
- **Iowa Farm & Land / realtor land sale databases and auction results** —
  recent comparable sales; adjust by CSR2.

## Community intelligence

- **County economic development offices** and Iowa Economic Development
  Authority certified sites inventory.
- **County supervisors' meeting minutes** (published online) — search for
  prior wind/solar/BESS agenda items to read the county's temperature
  before anyone makes a call.
